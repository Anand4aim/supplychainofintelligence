`/framework` is organized around the six questions a reader asks, in this exact order. Never reorder, never rename.

1. **Definition** — What is it? One sentence, evergreen. CanonicalDefinition + link to /paper.
2. **Map** — Where does value accrue? 10 layers, 50 sublayers, three tiers (Substrate / Workflow / Surface), gold-mining analogy, agent decoder, Three Currents. Currents live HERE, not in Laws.
3. **Laws** — Why does it work? The 4 structural Laws + JTBD vs SCoI table. Laws only — instruments do NOT live here.
4. **Dynamics** — How does it evolve? Six structural patterns under the Laws (renamed from "Six Observations") + Six Archetypes. Patterns earn promotion to Laws over time.
5. **Applications** — How do I use it? Two instruments (Defensible Triangle, Intelligence Cube) + four self-serve tools (/audit, /market-map, /playbook, /predictions). Framed as tools you run on yourself — never as services. L-1A compliance: no "hire me / work with me / engage" language.
6. **Observations** — What's happening now? Time-bound readings. Pointers to /live, /predictions, /case-studies. The ONLY section that carries dates.

**Tier grouping (canonical):**
- Substrate = L−1, L0, L1, L2, L3, L8 — depend-on. Compounds in years.
- Workflow  = L4, L5, L6              — live-inside. Survives in months.
- Surface   = L7                       — touch. Commoditizes in weeks.

Never use "Tier 0…Tier 4" or "Register" or "Knowledge Tiers" — that scaffolding is retired. KnowledgeTiers.tsx no longer exists.

**Instruments vs Laws:** The 4 Laws are falsifiable structural claims. The Defensible Triangle and Intelligence Cube™ are INSTRUMENTS — tools that place a company on the map. Instruments belong in Applications (section 5), never in Laws.
