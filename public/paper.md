## Abstract

Every generative-AI product sits on a 10-layer stack: **L-1 Resources, L0 Infrastructure, L1 Data, L2 Models, L3 Gatekeeping, L4 Access, L5 Execution, L6 Orchestration, L7 Surface, L8 Memory.** Four structural laws govern how value moves through that stack. Five observations describe the patterns that recur under those laws. The **Defensible Triangle** — L1b Proprietary Data + L5 Deep Skills & Playbooks + L8 Compounding Memory — is the most common application-layer fortress. This document is the canonical reference; please cite it directly.

---

## 1. The 10 layers

- **L-1 Resources** — energy, water, fabs, materials, skilled trades. The real bottleneck.
- **L0 Infrastructure** — silicon, data centers, interconnect, cloud, edge. The shovels.
- **L1 Data** — public, proprietary (L1b), behavioral/sensor (L1c), outcome (L1d), synthetic/simulation.
- **L2 Models** — foundation/multimodal, fine-tuned, embedding/retrieval, routing, reasoning & world models.
- **L3 Gatekeeping** — compliance, quality, safety/provenance, editorial, distribution gates.
- **L4 Access** — APIs, agent protocols (MCP), governance & agent commerce, real-time, agent identity.
- **L5 Execution** — domain execution, decision frameworks, RAG, playbooks, interaction skills & actuation.
- **L6 Orchestration** — agent loops, human-in-the-loop, role routing, state, runtime assurance.
- **L7 Surface** — conversational, visual, embedded/embodied, transactional, ambient.
- **L8 Memory** — session, entity, network learning, institutional, learned world models.

Full sublayer definitions live at https://supplychainofai.com/framework and per-layer pages at `/framework/{layer-id}`.

## 2. The 4 structural laws

**Law I — Intelligence Commoditizes Downward.** If a product depends only on generic model capability, the platform layer beneath it eventually absorbs the value. Wrappers don't survive — wrappers become features.
*Example:* Jasper fell from a $1.5B valuation to roughly $300M once ChatGPT shipped the same capability as a default.
Essay: https://supplychainofai.com/laws/intelligence-commoditizes-downward

**Law II — Value Accrues at Bottlenecks.** Durable value rarely sits in the model or the UI. It sits at the scarce layer — proprietary data (L1b), workflow control (L5), verification (L3), distribution (L4), memory (L8c–e), compliance (L3a).
*Examples:* NVIDIA owns L0 silicon. Vanta owns L3 compliance. Bloomberg owns L1b proprietary financial data.
Essay: https://supplychainofai.com/laws/value-accrues-at-bottlenecks

**Law III — The Surface Captures Attention; the Chain Captures Power.** A beautiful UI may win users. Durable companies own a deeper layer of the chain. Surface without depth is a graveyard.
*Example:* Gamma owns only L7 and remains exposed. Replit owns L4 + L5 + L6 + L8 and compounds.
Essay: https://supplychainofai.com/laws/surface-captures-attention-chain-captures-power

**Law IV — Generation and Verification Must Be Separate.** Wherever output carries fiduciary, regulatory, safety, or reputational weight, the generator (L2/L5) and the verifier (L3) must be separate economic entities.
*Examples:* Vanta sits above AWS. Snyk sits above Copilot. Big-4 audit sits above SAP. FDA sits above Pfizer.
Essay: https://supplychainofai.com/laws/generation-and-verification-must-be-separate

## 3. The Defensible Triangle

The most common application-layer fortress: **L1b Proprietary Data + L5 Deep Skills & Playbooks + L8 Compounding Memory.** Two of three is a workflow product. Three of three is an intelligence gate.

---

## 4. On the word "agent"

"Agent" is **not a layer.** It is marketing for an **L5 + L7 (+ sometimes L8)** package. When a company launches an agent, decode it: what other layers do they own?

- Agent + L1 = fortress (Sierra, Harvey).
- Agent + L4 = railroad (Salesforce Agentforce, Microsoft Copilot).
- Agent + nothing = exposed wrapper that commoditizes the moment the underlying L2 ships the same loop.
