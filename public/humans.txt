/* FRAMEWORK */
Name: The Supply Chain of Intelligence™
Structure: 10 Layers · 50 Sublayers · 3 Structural Laws · The Intelligence Cube™ · AI Defensibility Audit

The 10 Layers:
  L-1 Resources       — energy, water, fabs, materials, skilled trades
  L0  Infrastructure  — silicon, data centers, interconnect, cloud, edge
  L1  Data            — public, proprietary, behavioral, outcome, synthetic
  L2  Models          — foundation, specialized, embedding, routing, reasoning
  L3  Gatekeeping     — compliance, quality, safety, editorial, distribution
  L4  Access          — APIs, agent protocols (MCP), governance, real-time pipes, agent identity
  L5  Execution       — domain skills, decision frameworks, RAG, playbooks, interactional skills
  L6  Orchestration   — agent loops, human-in-the-loop, role routing, context state, runtime assurance
  L7  Surface         — conversational, visual, embedded/copilot, transactional, ambient
  L8  Memory          — session, entity, network, institutional, world-model

The 3 Structural Laws:
  I.   Intelligence commoditizes downward.        Wrappers become features.
  II.  Value accrues at bottlenecks.              Own the scarce layer.
  III. The surface captures attention; the chain captures power.

The Defensible Triangle: L1b proprietary data + L5a/b/d deep skills/playbooks + L8c/d/e compounding memory.
Single-layer survival examples: Vanta on L3 (gates), NVIDIA on L0 (silicon), Snowflake on L4 (pipes).

The AI Defensibility Audit:
  Eight 1–5 questions on model dependency, data ownership, workflow depth, trust gate,
  distribution, memory, switching cost, platform exposure. Bands: Thin Wrapper · Useful Tool, Weak
  Moat · Workflow Product · Defensible AI System · Intelligence Gate.

Built for: SaaS founders, AI product leaders, and venture investors auditing portfolios for AI exposure.
